ArchCam Support

Help with straighter lines, balanced framing, wide-angle photos, and top-down capture.

Getting started

Allow access

ArchCam needs Camera access to compose photos and add-only Photos access to save them. Motion access drives leveling and line correction. Location is optional and is used only for photo metadata.

Choose adjustments

Verticals straightens upright lines. Wide reduces edge stretching. Preview shows adjustments live; when it is off, the white outline shows the adjusted frame.

Save your photo

ArchCam always saves the adjusted photo. In Settings, you can keep a second uncorrected copy and choose HEIF or JPEG output.

Use the shutter

Tap the shutter button or press either iPhone volume button. ArchCam enables the next capture as soon as the camera is ready.

Troubleshooting

  • No camera image: open iPhone Settings, select ArchCam, and enable Camera access.
  • Photos are not saving: confirm Photos access is enabled for ArchCam in iPhone Settings.
  • The adjusted frame is smaller: perspective correction needs room around the subject. Step back or select a wider camera when available.
  • Live preview feels slow: turn Preview off. ArchCam will show the future adjusted-frame outline while retaining the native camera preview.

Contact support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, ArchCam version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send private photos unless they are necessary to explain the issue.
